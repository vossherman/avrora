Clip2Tri Polygon Triangulation Library, version commit f62a734d22733814b8a970ed8a68a4d94c24fa5f





Clip2Tri can be used together with Clipper for robust triangulation.


Used in the QML plugin of Qt Location and in Qt Positioning.


The sources can be found in qtpositioning/src/3rdparty/clip2tri.
