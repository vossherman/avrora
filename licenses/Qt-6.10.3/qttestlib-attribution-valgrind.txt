Valgrind, version 3.25.1





An instrumentation framework for building dynamic analysis tools.


Used on Linux ond MacOS in the Qt Test module.


The sources can be found in qtbase/src/testlib/3rdparty/valgrind/valgrind_p.h and qtbase/src/testlib/3rdparty/valgrind/callgrind_p.h.
