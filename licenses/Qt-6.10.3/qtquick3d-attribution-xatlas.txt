xatlas, version 16ace528acd2cf1f16a7c0dde99c42c486488dbe





xatlas is a small C++11 library with no external dependencies that generates unique texture coordinates suitable for baking lightmaps or texture painting.


Used for lightmap UV set generation in Qt Quick 3D


The sources can be found in qtquick3d/src/3rdparty/xatlas.
