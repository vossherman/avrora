Poly2Tri Polygon Triangulation Library, version commit a922022da4c0d3a55b1d6b6d634219caea653053





Poly2Tri is a sweepline constrained Delaunay Polygon Triangulation Library.


Used in the QML plugin of Qt Location and in Qt Positioning.


The sources can be found in qtpositioning/src/3rdparty/poly2tri.
